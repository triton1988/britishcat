British Cat Privacy Policy

Effective: 7 November 2026

British Cat is an iPhone and iPad game in which you learn English together with a cat. This policy is written by the app's developer, Viktor Kalinichenko ("we"). In short: we do not collect your personal data, we have no servers of our own that hold it, we show no ads, use no analytics or tracking, and sell nothing to third parties.

What is stored on your device

  • What you enter in the game: your name, age, gender and English level; your cat's name and sex.
  • Progress: finished stories, scores, coins, room items, achievements, settings.

All of this is kept only in the game's save file on your device. The Home Screen widget reads a short snapshot of the cat's state from the same device.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is turned on, the save file is synced through the iCloud storage of your own Apple account so your progress appears on your other devices. This data is held by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; we have no access to it.

Friends and the cats' race

If you invite a friend, the race and your messages are kept in a shared area of your own iCloud, which only you and that friend can open. Your friend sees your name, your cat's name, how far your cat has run and the messages you send. We have no access to it. Removing the friend deletes the shared area.

Microphone and speech recognition

For the "Say it in English" exercise the app asks for microphone and speech-recognition access. Where the device supports it, recognition runs on the device itself. Otherwise your answer is processed by Apple's speech recognition service under Apple's terms. We do not record or receive either the sound or the text of your answers. The exercise can be switched off in the game's settings, and access can be revoked in iOS Settings.

Purchases

The "Premium forever" purchase is made through the App Store; payment is handled by Apple and we never see your card details. To check whether you own Premium, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives purchase information from the App Store, a random anonymous identifier created by the app, and technical data (device model, iOS version, store country). It does not receive your name, age or e-mail. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Notifications

Reminders from the cat are local notifications scheduled by the device itself. They do not pass through our servers. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in iOS Settings.

Crash reports

iOS may give the app reports about crashes and hangs (Apple MetricKit). The app keeps the last 10 such reports on the device only. We see a report only if you choose to send it from Settings. If you have agreed in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may also give us anonymised crash data.

Children

The game suits all ages and has a separate kids mode. It contains no outbound links, and a parental gate appears before a purchase, restoring purchases or any link. We collect no data about children. A child's name and age stay on the device only.

Deleting your data

"Delete all progress" in Settings erases the save on this device and in iCloud. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from the device. We keep no copies, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your rights

We do not process your personal data on our servers. You can therefore access, correct or delete it yourself in the app. For data held by RevenueCat or Apple, we will help you reach them.

Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.

Contact

Privacy questions: tritonbios@gmail.com.

このゲームはすべての年齢に適しており、専用のキッズモードがあります。キッズモードには外部へのリンクがなく、購入・購入の復元・リンクの前には保護者向けの確認が表示されます。私たちは子どもに関するデータを収集しません。子どもの名前と年齢は端末内にだけ残ります。

データの削除

設定の「進行状況をすべて削除」で、この端末と iCloud のセーブデータが消去されます。アプリを削除すると、端末からそのデータもすべて削除されます。私たちはコピーを持っていないため、こちら側で削除するものはありません。

あなたの権利

私たちはあなたの個人データをサーバーで処理しません。そのため、確認・訂正・削除はアプリ内でご自身で行えます。RevenueCat や Apple が保管するデータについては、連絡のお手伝いをします。

変更

アプリのデータの扱いが変わる場合は、そのバージョンの公開前にこのページと上記の日付を更新します。
